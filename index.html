Our services

General & cosmetic dentistry, under one roof

From regular cleanings and exams for the whole family to Invisalign treatment, cosmetic procedures, emergency appointments and more, we use the latest technology to leave you smiling.

General Dentistry

Cleanings, exams and X-rays, dental fillings, porcelain crowns, preventative care and pediatric dentistry for the whole family.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Porcelain veneers, smile makeovers, teeth bonding, gum contouring and BioClear — a smile you’ll want to show off.

Dental Implants

Single tooth, multiple tooth, All-on-4 and All-on-6 implants, plus implant repair and implant-supported dentures.

Invisalign & Orthodontics

Invisalign clear aligners, braces and Vivera retainers — straighter teeth without metal brackets.

Zoom Teeth Whitening

Professional Zoom whitening for a brighter smile in a single visit.

Emergency Dentistry

Tooth pain, a broken tooth or a lost filling — call us for an emergency appointment and we will get you seen fast.

Also available: laser dentistry, sleep apnea treatment, sedation dentistry, root canal therapy, oral surgery, dentures and second opinions — ask us about your smile.
